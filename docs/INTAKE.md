## 7. Images

All to Drive. **Real photographs only** — no stock, no AI-generated. On a charity appeal,
fake imagery is a trust liability, and it is usually spotted.

Every image needs a caption: **what it shows, where, and when**.

| Use | Folder | Size (min) | Ratio | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hero / campaign | `/hero/` | 2400×1600 | 3:2 | 3–5 options |
| People | `/people/` | 1200×900 | 4:3 | head and shoulders, plain background |
| Partner logos | `/partners/` | 1000px wide | any | SVG or transparent PNG |
| Evidence / on-the-day | `/evidence/` | 1600×1200 | 4:3 or 3:2 | as many as you have |
| Social share card | `/social/` | 1200×630 | 1.91:1 | optional, we generate one otherwise |
| Logo | `/brand/` | SVG | — | plus a square version for the favicon |

JPG or PNG. Under 5MB each. Don't crop or filter them — we apply one grade across all of them.
